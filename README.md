# Lab 02: Place Perspectives - Seattle

Research Narrative
**Perspectives within, outside, and between Seattle.**
- **Location used: Seattle**, (WA 47.6061° N, 122.3328° W)
- **Data Collection Date:** January 21st, 2026
- Search Terms consisted of "Visit Seattle", "Moving To Seattle", "Living in Seattle"
- This project explores the difference between three search terms regarding Seattle:
- **Seattle as a "commodity"** (a product sold to tourists) 
- **Seattle as a "community"** (the reality of the city and its people).
- **The Transition** Between the two dynamics is the transitioning aspect, "Moving to Seattle".


- My topic consists of comparing Seattle from three perspectives: visting as a tourists, seeing the city from an outsiders perspective, versus living within the city. My third perspective is from within a transitory state, I was curious to see the terms of individuals in the process of relocating to the city.
- This project attempts to acknowledge the three perspectives and how they differ. This project was intended to decipher patterns between perspectives of the city Seattle as a community, transition, and commodity.
    
**Comparison of Word Clouds:**
-A obvious similarity between the three word clouds icludes the word "Rain" which Seattle is well known for. 
-Another similarity I found between: "Living in Seattle" and "Moving to Seattle" was the mentioning of the term "Friends" and "Alone" often. As the city is known for it's "Freeze", I can understand why this term would pop up as a potential concern when speaking from a soon to be resident/residents' perspective.
    
**Possible Reasons for Observed Patterns:**
- As a strategy to garner views, social media influencers will lend to their titles, the term "Best" to summarize the city and its gems.
- There was a large attention towards the terms, "Pros & Cons", this best captures the reality of the city for individuals who are in the process of making large financial decisions.

**Future Improvements:**    
- If I had to repeat this, I would scrape more results, to get a large data pool to pull from.
- Secondly I would block potential "noise" words such as "Subscribe", "Vlog", "Channel", these words add little significance to the word clouds and exits as redundancies.

**Expectations vs. After:**
- My expectations before the report from "Visting Seattle" is to see Landmark Locations, Food spots, Hotels, Guides etc. Whereas for the "Living in Seattle" crowd, I expect to see the Cost, Rent, Neighborhoods, Rain, Pros & Cons, Tech, Safety. "Moving to Seattle" will likely mention apartments, prices, housing, neighborhood key words.
- The data largely met my expectations, however, a surprising factor was the prominence of social terms such as "Friends",  "Alone". This stood out from the other terms as it highlighted the social cost of living besides the constantly discussed financial cost.
